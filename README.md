# Afterbook

**Live at [afterbook.app](https://afterbook.app).**

Cash close vs the Aero book, in shares, for the Coinbase tokenized stocks on Base. The tape, Lot Lab and My Lots are read-only — no wallet ever signs anything there, and execution stays on [Aerodrome](https://aerodrome.finance). The one exception is **Baskets** (`/baskets`): a non-custodial basket product run on [Glider](https://glider.fi), where *you* sign an enrollment and withdrawals and send USDC from your own wallet. See "Baskets (Basis Tilt)" below.

**Pages:** `/` and `/[symbol]` (the tape for a stock), `/today` ("Biggest gap"), `/baskets`, `/baskets/risks`.

## What it shows

- **Tape**: cash-market last price vs Aerodrome's on-chain mid price for the twelve Coinbase tokenized stocks Afterbook tracks (NVDAc, AAPLc, METAc, GOOGLc, AMZNc, MSFTc, MSTRc, SNDKc, SPCXc, TSLAc, plus PLTRc and MUc added 2026-10-01), basis in bp, plus real pool depth. Each row is a card with the token's icon, a 24h change and a past-N-days chart (see "UI architecture"). During pre-market and after-hours, "cash" is the live extended-hours print (Yahoo's `preMarketPrice`/`postMarketPrice`), not the frozen 4pm close — comparing on-chain against a stale close would misreport a real overnight tradfi move as basis. While cash is closed, the hero's gap number and sentence (and `/today`) stay anchored to the actual regular-session close, not the live extended-hours print, so the home page and `/today` never disagree about the same instant. In pre-market/after-hours the hero labels the cash price by the row's real `cashPriceType`: only when Yahoo has an extended-hours print does it say "cash pre-market/after-hours price"; with no print the cash price is still the last close, so it says "Friday's close" and the card says "Cash close". The tape's cash column header and the note under "Tape" follow the same rule, computed from all rows: no row on a print → "Cash close" and "No pre-market trades yet, so the basis is measured against the last regular-session close"; every row on a print → "Cash pre-market/after-hours"; a mix → "Cash (latest)" and a note saying it is a live print where there is one and the last close otherwise. Link-preview, share-card and footer copy use plain language, not the "Aero book" jargon. A symbol reporting earnings within 5 days gets a small "Earnings in Nd" flag in the tape and a caveat in Lot Lab — a widened spread near earnings is expected, not a sign the tape's basis math is broken.
- **Liquid vs thin split**: pools with real depth (≥ $1M; at the time of writing NVDAc/AAPLc/METAc/GOOGLc/AMZNc/MSTRc/SNDKc/SPCXc — MSFTc, TSLAc, PLTRc and MUc are thin) lead the tape and hero grid; everything thinner collapses behind a "Show N thin books" toggle at reduced size. A $10k pool's basis swings hundreds of bp on noise alone — giving it the same visual weight as a $2M pool made the tape read as broken. $100k used to be the bar, but `depthUsd` is a pool's total real token balance, not liquidity active near the current tick — a pool can clear $100k on stale/off-range positions while still swinging hundreds of bp (MSTRc did, at ~$965k depth and +257bp), so the bar moved to where the pools actually separate into a calm tier and a still-noisy one. The split is driven by live depth, not a hardcoded symbol list, so a thin pool graduates automatically once it actually has liquidity. Landing directly on a thin symbol (URL or dropdown) gets a small "thin book · $Xk depth" chip under the sticky bar, since the hero/tape's own thin-tier demotion is otherwise the only clue.
- **Hero card + compact bar**: the first thing on the tape page. It opens on the day's biggest liquid gap (the same pick `/today` uses, chosen on the server in `app/page.tsx`; `/NVDAc` etc. still open on that stock). It holds the stock chooser, the market-session pill, the big gap number with one plain sentence ("Strategy (MSTR) is trading 2.7% (268.2 bp) above Friday's close on Aerodrome. Cash reopens Mon 9:30 AM ET.": percent first, because strangers read it faster, bp in brackets; under 5 bp only the bp is shown, since the percent would round to 0.00% and look like a mistake: `gapSizeText` in `lib/format.ts`, also used for the share text), cash and Aero price cards, a primary "Size a SYMBOL trade" button (scrolls to Lot Lab, where the eligibility check lives; it never opens Aerodrome directly), a Telegram alerts link (to `t.me/afterbook_bot`; the bot's display name is "AfterbookAlertsBot", which is not its username), and a one-time note defining Aero and "100 bp = 1%". While cash is closed the number is anchored to the actual close (same as `/today`), not a live extended-hours print. A compact bar with the same number sticks to the top once the hero scrolls away. The page's `<h1>` is visually hidden.
- **No tile grid:** an earlier version repeated every liquid stock as a clickable tile under the hero (the same numbers as the ticker and the table). It was removed; the tape rows below are the way to switch stock, and clicking one still jumps to Lot Lab.
- **Basis since close**: a sparkline of the selected symbol's basis from the last cash close to now, sampled opportunistically off real traffic into Redis (no cron job) — gaps fill in as the app keeps running, and it works with zero setup (just renders empty) if no Redis store is configured. Recorded for every tracked stock regardless of the liquid/thin split above — it briefly reused that same $1M gate and silently stopped recording six symbols' history until that got decoupled; a thin pool's basis history is at least as useful to someone deciding whether to trade it. The same 30-day retained history also powers two one-line stats underneath, once there's enough sampled history: mean/max basis while the cash market was closed, and an **open-snap** convergence stat (`getOpenSnapStats`, `lib/history.ts`) — for each trading day, compares the last sample before that day's 9:30am NY open against samples ~30/90 min after, and reports how often |basis| shrank rather than widened ("basis moved toward $0 within 30 min in X% of the last N sessions"). Requires at least 8 valid trading days in the window before showing anything, same "collecting data" honesty as the sparkline itself rather than a percentage computed off too few days.
- **NY session clock**: cash-open / pre-market / after-hours / weekend / holiday state, so the 24/7-onchain-vs-24/5-cash gap is explicit, not implied.
- **Lot Lab**: type a USDC amount (or pick a size preset — $250 / $1k / $5k / 1% of the pool), see shares out, execution price, and a price-impact curve across a log-spaced range of trade sizes ($500–$1M) — computed from the pool's live `slot0()`/`liquidity()`, not a cached reserve snapshot. The panel is split into **Trade / LP / Carry** tabs (Trade is the default) rather than stacking trade sizing, the depth chart, and the carry math into one long card — keeps the primary execution flow front and center and makes the tool readable on mobile. While cash is closed and the basis spread exceeds 20bp, also shows a cash-and-carry edge calculator (`lib/arb.ts`) in its own Carry tab: gross basis, pool fee, price impact, and a flat gas estimate netted out, then annualized over the actual time remaining until the cash market reopens — the tab itself is hidden outside that window rather than shown with nothing worth acting on. Still buy-only in the sense that this app never shorts or borrows, but the Carry tab now has both directions: a **buy basis** side (on-chain priced *below* cash — the classic setup) and a **short basis / inventory-unwind** side (on-chain priced *above* cash — for someone who already holds spot shares and wants to sell into that premium now, then rebuy the same notional in cash pre-market once it reopens). `computeCashAndCarryEdge` (`lib/arb.ts`) takes a `direction` param that mirrors the sign; the toggle defaults to whichever side actually has an edge for the active symbol's current basis. A **delta-neutral hedge** note below the calculator explains pairing either side with a same-notional perp position to strip out the stock's own price risk — links to real, individually-verified venues (Hyperliquid, Avantis, Synthetix) for checking live funding, not a live integration this app computes itself (no third-party price/data API, on purpose — see "Why the math is done the way it is"). Also shows the same amount priced as a full-range LP position (≈50/50 by value — exact for full-range concentrated liquidity, with a caveat that Aerodrome defaults new deposits to a narrower range). Below that, a liquidity-depth chart (`lib/depth.ts`) reconstructs active liquidity across a ±25% price window from the pool's own tick-bitmap/`ticks()` data — no subgraph, no indexer. The chart doubles as an interactive LP range selector: drag its two handles to size a candidate range and see, live, its capital efficiency (`lib/lpRange.ts` — derived from the same virtual-reserve equations as the impact curve, not a recalled formula), an estimated fee APR (the pool's own trailing feeGrowth-based APR times that multiplier), the probability the range is still in range at the same 7-day horizon My Lots uses for real positions, and a **divergence-loss-at-boundary** figure (`computeDivergenceLossAtBoundary`, `lib/lpRange.ts`) — the standard concentrated-liquidity impermanent-loss benchmark if price reaches exactly the low or high edge, verified against the classic Uniswap V2 IL formula in the full-range limit before trusting it. Dragging itself is gated behind an explicit "Adjust range" toggle, off by default — the range stays visible either way, but a finger landing on the chart on mobile scrolls the page instead of getting captured as a drag unless that toggle is on. Handles clamp to each other and to the current price, so a dragged range always brackets it — the underlying math assumes that. Below the range metrics, an **auto-recenter** note explains what re-centering an out-of-range position actually involves and links to Gelato Web3 Functions and Bankr for building that automation yourself — Afterbook doesn't run it, for the same reason it doesn't construct swap calldata (see "Why the math is done the way it is" above). A "Copy trade" button copies the sized trade as plain text. When a symbol has real earnings-move history (see below), Lot Lab also shows an **earnings volatility spread**: how many days of the stock's realized volatility the pool's own liquidity concentration (`lib/lpRange.ts`'s highest-density-region construction) would justify, compared to the actual days until the next report and the historical average size of this stock's post-earnings moves (`lib/earningsHistory.ts`). Not implied volatility in the options-market sense — there's no options market on these pools — a liquidity-concentration proxy translated into a comparable "days" unit via the same realized-vol model used elsewhere, not a fabricated number.
- **My Lots**: a wallet chip, summary cards (total / spot / Aerodrome LP value) and one card per holding (spot as icon, USD value and share of holdings; LP with an in-range badge and range bar). Connect a wallet — a RainbowKit picker showing every installed browser-extension wallet (Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, etc., detected live via EIP-6963) plus a manual fallback, read-only — the connection only reveals your public address, nothing is ever signed — to see spot share balances and Aerodrome LP positions across the tracked stocks, priced live. Shows your [Basename](https://www.base.org/names) instead of the raw address when one's set, resolved directly against Base's own L2Resolver — no separate mainnet RPC, no extra dependency. LP position data comes from Aerodrome/Velodrome's own "Sugar" read-helper contract (`positionsByFactory`), not the publicly-documented NFT position manager — that one is bound to a different CL factory than these specific pools use and would silently show nothing. Each LP position also shows its price range with in-range/out-of-range status, fees and AERO emissions earned, and lock expiry if locked — all fields Sugar already returns on the same call, just surfaced rather than discarded. The range is now also a visual bar marking where the current price sits, plus two estimates: fee APR (from daily on-chain snapshots of the pool's own `feeGrowthGlobal0X128`/`feeGrowthGlobal1X128` counters — exact pool-wide fee revenue over the snapshot window, not a third-party volume API) and the odds the price is still in range 7 days out (from realized volatility of recently-sampled on-chain price, via a standard lognormal estimate). Both are on-chain-only by design — no third-party price/volume API. In-range odds still start as "collecting data" until enough samples exist, but fee APR no longer does: `computeFeeAprWithFallback` (`lib/feeApr.ts`) falls back to summing real Swap-event volume over the most recent ~1900 blocks (confirmed live: mainnet.base.org caps `eth_getLogs` at a 2,000-block range) and extrapolating that window's fee-to-TVL ratio, whenever there isn't yet enough feeGrowthGlobal snapshot history — verified end-to-end against real chain data for three live pools before trusting it. Labeled "prelim." in the UI so it doesn't read as the same-confidence real figure. My Lots also shows a **gauge-vs-fee yield comparison** (`lib/gaugeYield.ts`): the AERO staking APR this pool's gauge would pay next to the unstaked fee APR above, so the panel says outright which currently earns more. AERO's own USD price is read from a real on-chain Aerodrome pool (found via the CL factory's `getPool`, not hardcoded) — this app previously had no AERO price source at all and showed emissions as a raw token amount for exactly that reason; that gap's closed now. Staked liquidity's USD value can't use the same virtual-reserve math the impact curve does (that describes trade-impact behavior, not actual holdings, and overstates a concentrated position's real value) — instead it scales the pool's real, balance-based depth by the staked/total liquidity ratio, an approximation that assumes staked and unstaked positions are concentrated similarly on average. A position also gets flagged when the most recent epoch's veAERO voting incentives (bribes, from Aerodrome's `RewardsSugar.epochsByAddress`) outpaid real trading fees for that same pool/epoch — a signal that a pool's gauge APR may be governance-subsidized rather than earned from organic volume.

## Why the math is done the way it is

The stock/USDC pools are Aerodrome **Slipstream** (concentrated-liquidity, Uniswap-V3-style) pools, not classic constant-product pools — confirmed on-chain (`stable()`/`getReserves()` revert, `slot0()`/`liquidity()`/`tickSpacing()` work). Aerodrome's documented public Quoter and SwapRouter are bound to a *different* CL factory than the one these pools were deployed through (`pool.factory() != quoter.factory()`, verified on-chain), so the standard Quoter reverts on these pools and can't be used here.

Instead, `lib/quote.ts` reads `slot0()` and `liquidity()` directly and uses the fact that a CL pool behaves exactly like a constant-product pool with virtual reserves `(L / sqrtP, L * sqrtP)` as long as a trade stays within the current tick's liquidity — that's exact math, not an approximation, but it stops being exact once a trade is large enough to cross into a different tick range (flagged as `largeTradeCaveat`, and shaded on the impact curve). This is explicitly framed in the UI as an estimate for sizing a trade, not a firm quote — the actual fill always happens on Aerodrome's own app.

Pool depth (the tape's Depth column, and the liquid/thin split) comes from real `token.balanceOf(pool)` reads — deliberately *not* the virtual reserves used for impact math, which describe in-range liquidity depth and run much larger than what's actually deployed.

All token and pool addresses in `lib/tokens.ts` were verified on-chain (`decimals()`, `token0()`/`token1()`, `fee()`, `factory()`) and cross-checked against Dexscreener's Aerodrome pair listings — the original four on 2026-09-04, plus AMZNc/MSFTc/MSTRc/SNDKc/SPCXc/TSLAc on 2026-09-05 when Base expanded to ten tokenized stocks. All ten share the same CL factory, 0.05% fee tier, and tick spacing. PLTRc and MUc were added on 2026-10-01 after being matched against Coinbase's own public list (`api.coinbase.com/v1/tokenized-stocks`: contract address, decimals, `multiplier()`), checked on-chain (USDC is `token0`, fee 500, tick spacing 10) and against Dexscreener's Aerodrome listings; both pools hold roughly $0.6M, so they sit in the thin tier. Liquidity on the newer six is much thinner ($10k–$110k vs $1–2M on the original four) — that's why the tape splits them out, and why their impact curves and `largeTradeCaveat` trip at far smaller sizes.

Each stock token also implements the [B20 asset standard](https://docs.base.org/get-started/issue-rwa)'s `multiplier()` — a fixed-point scalar the issuer can change without migrating raw balances. This isn't split-only, despite how this file used to describe it: per Base's own B20 spec, cash dividends are reinvested into additional shares by raising the same multiplier, net of withholding tax and a Coinbase fee. All tracked tokens read 1.0x today, but `lib/quote.ts` reads it live on every quote rather than assuming 1.0 forever, so "shares out" stays correct the moment either happens — and `lib/corporateActions.ts` watches for exactly that: a Telegram ping to the maintainer whenever a symbol's multiplier changes from its last known value, since that's a real corporate action worth a human glance. The active stock's page also shows the redemption ratio directly whenever it's not 1.0x.

On-chain reads go through `https://mainnet.base.org`, Base's documented official RPC endpoint, with a public third-party RPC as a fallback.

Earnings dates come from Nasdaq's public, undocumented `api.nasdaq.com/api/calendar/earnings` endpoint — no key, same "open but unofficial" trust shape as the Yahoo endpoint above. Yahoo's own calendar endpoint (`quoteSummary` + `calendarEvents`, which used to return this) now needs a crumb+cookie handshake this app doesn't have, so it wasn't an option. A ticker with no date on file (expect this for SPCX, which only IPO'd in mid-2026) just shows nothing, not an error.

`lib/earningsHistory.ts` separately accumulates *past* reports (the calendar endpoint above only ever holds the next upcoming date, overwritten as time passes) — for each report, the % move from the last close before it to the first close on or after the day after, from Yahoo's historical daily-close data (same host, a longer `period1`/`period2` window instead of `range=1d`). Seeded with a one-time ~400-day backfill; the daily earnings-sync cron extends it by a few days' lag going forward, so the sample grows every quarter. A symbol with no reports yet in the window just shows no historical-average stat, not a fabricated one.

## Baskets (Basis Tilt)

`/baskets` offers **Afterbook Basis Tilt**: a basket of ten Base tokenized stocks (NVDAc, AAPLc, METAc, GOOGLc, AMZNc, MSFTc, MSTRc, SNDKc, SPCXc, TSLAc) weighted by what the on-chain books show instead of equally or by market cap. It runs on [Glider](https://glider.fi)'s B2B API: the user's funds sit in a smart account they own on Base, Glider's automation rebalances it (swaps route through LI.FI to Aerodrome), and Afterbook only chooses the target weights. Afterbook never holds funds, keys or executes trades for baskets. Not available in the US (the same best-effort, fail-closed `x-vercel-ip-country` gate as the rest of the app, applied to the page *and* to every `/api/baskets/*` route). No Afterbook fee.

**User flow** (`app/components/BasketPanel.tsx`): connect wallet → review and sign one message (creates the smart account; no funds move) → send USDC from the wallet to the account (an in-app plain ERC-20 transfer with a balance check and Max) → Glider buys the basket → withdraw any time, as USDC (Glider sells) or as the tokens, with an EIP-712 signature that expires in 10 minutes. Outcomes also show as toasts, and the withdrawal status is polled from Glider's operation endpoint. Enrolling requires ticking an acknowledgement linking to `/baskets/risks`.

**Weights** (`lib/baskets/weights.ts`, pure): half follows real pool depth, half follows |cash-vs-chain basis| counted only for liquid pools (≥ $1M; a thin pool's basis is mostly noise). Each name is bounded to 2–25%; bounds are enforced by scaling the raw scores with a bisection so the weights always sum to exactly 1, then rounded to 2-decimal percent strings that sum to exactly `100` (largest-remainder), which is the format Glider requires. `lib/baskets/tilt.ts` turns the tape into weights and CAIP-19 asset ids (`eip155:8453/erc20:0x…`).

**The basket universe is explicit.** `BASKET_SYMBOLS` in `lib/baskets/tilt.ts` is the ten names above and is deliberately separate from the tracked list in `lib/tokens.ts`: adding a stock to the tape must never silently change what enrolled users hold. Widening the basket is a deliberate decision plus a new strategy version.

**Smoothing** (`smoothWeights`): the published weights follow an exponential moving average (alpha 0.3) of the target, and a new Glider strategy version is published only when at least **10%** of the basket would change hands (`MIN_TURNOVER`; it was 5% until 2026-10-07, when a replay of 19 days of stored history showed 5% would have changed the basket on about 2 days in 3, roughly 90 bp a month in trading cost at ~0.9% per unit moved, versus about 3 publishes and ~50 bp at 10%) — every published version makes every enrolled portfolio trade (about 0.9% round trip in a measured $5 test), so a noisy basis must not cause daily churn. The daily job (`/api/cron/basket-tilt`, 22:00 UTC) saves the moving average on every scheduled run, publishes only when `BASKET_AUTOPUBLISH=1`, and messages `TELEGRAM_ADMIN_CHAT_ID` each time it publishes; `?dry=1` reports the plan, writes nothing, and shows `autopublishEnabled`.

**Strategy settings** (set once on the Glider strategy, not in code): private (`isPublic: false`), daily schedule, 1% slippage, 1% price impact, $1 minimum swap (Glider's own floor).

**Server routes** (`app/api/baskets/`): `enroll/signature`, `enroll`, `portfolio`, `start`, `withdraw/signature`, `withdraw`, `operation`. All go through `guardBasketRequest` (`lib/baskets/guard.ts`): fail-closed geo gate, config check (`GLIDER_API_KEY` and `GLIDER_STRATEGY_ID` both set — removing either is the off switch for the whole feature), then a Redis rate limiter that refuses when Redis is missing. The strategy and chain are fixed server-side, and a user's portfolio is always resolved from their owner address, never from a caller-supplied id. `lib/glider.ts` is the typed client (retries only on 429 and `API_506`; env values are trimmed because a stray space from a dashboard paste once broke enrollment on the live site).

**Daily job** (`/api/cron/basket-tilt`, 22:00 UTC): plans the next update and publishes only when `BASKET_AUTOPUBLISH=1` and `?dry=1` is not set; otherwise it just reports the plan and writes nothing. It refuses to touch a strategy whose asset list is not exactly the basket names. Auto-publish is **off** by default; weights have been published by hand while the smoothing is observed (see the `basis-tilt-ops` skill for the safe procedure).

**Environment variables**: `GLIDER_API_KEY` (a *production* tenant key — Glider's B2B API has no staging; test with separate keys. Production needs the six scopes `strategies:read/write`, `enroll:write`, `portfolios:read/write/withdraw`; a local dev key needs only `strategies:read`, `portfolios:read`, `enroll:write`), `GLIDER_API_BASE` (optional; defaults to `https://api.glider.fi/v2`), `GLIDER_STRATEGY_ID`, `BASKET_AUTOPUBLISH`, and `DEV_GEO_COUNTRY` (honoured only under `next dev`, so a developer machine can pass the fail-closed gate; a production build ignores it).

## Notifications

The bell in the header opens a small settings popover: a **Live market alerts** switch (on by default), a locked **Your transactions — always on** row, and a short Recent list. The choice is stored in `localStorage` only (no accounts), and the bell's dot is a status light — shown while live alerts are on.

- **Live market alerts** (`lib/liveAlerts.ts`, pure; `app/components/LiveAlerts.tsx` polls `/api/tape` every 60s while the tab is visible): a toast when a liquid pool's |gap| crosses 100 bp (it must fall back under 80 bp before it can fire again) or the cash market opens or closes. The first read after load only sets a baseline, so nothing fires for something already true. Alerts exist only while the site is open; Telegram covers the closed-tab case.
- **Transaction toasts** (`app/components/notify.tsx`): enroll, deposit, withdrawal progress and failures in the basket panel always show; the switch cannot turn them off.

## UI architecture

- **Themes** (`app/globals.css`): dark tokens live on `:root`, light is a token override on `:root[data-theme='light']`. A small inline script in `<head>` (`app/components/theme.ts`, nonce'd to satisfy the CSP) sets `data-theme` before first paint from the saved choice, else the OS setting. Hard-coded greys were replaced by tokens (`--inset`, `--surface-hover`, `--overlay-*`), and RainbowKit switches between its dark and light themes with the app.
- **Wallet button** (`WalletConnectButton.tsx`, used in the header, My Lots and Baskets): connected, it opens an account panel (address, wallet name, copy, Basescan link, network, Disconnect) and never disconnects on a bare click. The app is Base-only (`lib/wagmiConfig.ts` has one chain), so on any other network the button turns amber ("Wrong network") and the panel offers a one-click "Switch to Base". The component is split into a wagmi wrapper and a props-only `WalletButtonView` so every state can be rendered without a wallet.
- **Ticker strip** (`TickerStrip.tsx`): each item shows icon, symbol, Aero price and gap, so a stranger can tell which stock is which; items link to the stock's page.
- **Share button** (`ShareButton.tsx`): on the tape hero (next to "Size a … trade") and on `/today`, one identical standard secondary button (`.btn btn-secondary`) on both pages. It opens a small panel: "Share via…" (the browser's system share sheet, only where `navigator.share` exists), "Post on X" (an `x.com/intent/post` link with the hero sentence and the page URL), "Copy link", and "Download card" (the page's `opengraph-image`, the same PNG link previews use). The link is built from the page's own origin, so previews only work on the live site. **Every share gets its own URL** (`?s=<stamp>`, set when the panel opens): X caches a link's preview card by URL for about a week and the card shows live numbers, so a bare `/MSTRc` kept showing the first card ever fetched, contradicting the post's sentence (seen 2026-10-07: post said 91.8 bp, cached card said +278.6 bp from two days earlier). `robots.txt` disallows `/*?s=` so those URLs aren't indexed as duplicates. The text is the hero sentence's wording, facts only; keep it free of advice. The per-stock cards (`/<SYMBOL>/opengraph-image`) now carry the same icon and fade line as the `/today` card.
- **Brand mark:** a crescent (after hours) over two stepped lines (the order book). Defined once in `lib/brand.ts` and used by the header (`BrandMark.tsx`, themed: sky tile in dark mode, blue in light), the favicon (`app/icon.svg`, hand-copied from the same path), the iOS home-screen icon (`app/apple-icon.tsx`) and the share cards (`lib/ogImage.tsx`). If the mark changes, update `icon.svg` too.
- **Header** (`app/components/SiteHeader.tsx`): a full-width bar that condenses into a floating centred pill after about 32px of scroll (with hysteresis; desktop and tablet only — phones keep a two-row bar that scrolls away). The element keeps its height so nothing below shifts.
- **Token icons** (`lib/coinbaseIcons.ts`): the root layout reads Coinbase's public tokenized-stock list on the server (cached six hours), keeps an icon only when the entry's contract address matches `lib/tokens.ts` and the URL is on `metadata.coinbase.com`, and passes the URLs through context. `SymbolTile` renders the icon or a tinted letter tile (`lib/symbolStyle.ts`) when it is missing or fails. The icons are the companies' own logos served by Coinbase; the footer says they belong to their owners and Afterbook is not affiliated.
- **Trends** (`/api/sparklines`): one request for every row — up to 30 days of time-bucketed on-chain price samples plus the 24h change (reported only when a sample exists within 3 hours of 24 hours ago). All rows are drawn on one shared time axis whose length is the oldest stored sample (up to 30 days), and the tape header reads "Past Nd" with that real span, so it never claims 30 days before there are 30. A token with less than 3 days of its own history shows a small "New · Nd" label instead of a chart (a line stretched across the axis would pass for a long trend); from 3 days it draws only the part of the axis it covers. A token's 24h change uses the sample nearest to 24 hours ago within a 3-hour tolerance, so for a day-old token it is measured over slightly under 24 hours.
- **Form controls**: one zero-specificity rule styles every `select` and text input inside `<main>` (so components like the compact `.sticky-symbol-select` can still override it); the RainbowKit modal portals outside `<main>` and is untouched. Interactive elements without their own ring get a fallback `:focus-visible` outline.

## Security model

- Allowlisted contracts only — twelve token addresses, twelve pool addresses, all verified on-chain. The active stock's token and pool addresses are also shown directly in the Execute panel with BaseScan links, so you don't have to trust this README or leave the app to check what you're actually trading.
- All price/quote fetches happen server-side (Next.js route handlers); the browser only talks to this app's own `/api/*` routes.
- My Lots connects a wallet to *read* balances — no seed phrase ever touches this app, no signature or approval is ever requested there, no custom swap router, and this app never constructs swap calldata. Connecting only exposes the public address, which is already public on-chain regardless. **Baskets are different and say so:** enrolling asks the user to sign a message and withdrawing asks for an EIP-712 signature (both bound to the user's own account by Glider), and a deposit is a plain USDC transfer the user sends from their own wallet. Afterbook never holds funds or keys for baskets; Glider's automation can rebalance inside the strategy but, per Glider's docs, cannot withdraw without a fresh user signature.
- The non-US geofence (`x-vercel-ip-country`) is a best-effort UX gate, not a compliance control — it's defeated by any VPN, and the UI says so. Detected-US viewers see explicit "not available" copy rather than a silently-disabled button.
- Every execution link points at `aerodrome.finance` — Aerodrome's own app decides how to route the trade.
- Strict CSP (script-src limited to same-origin + a fresh per-request nonce — the tiny theme-init script in `<head>` carries that nonce), HSTS, X-Frame-Options: DENY, no third-party scripts or fonts (Poppins is self-hosted by `next/font`). The only third-party images are the token icons from `metadata.coinbase.com`, allowed in `img-src` and loaded with `referrerPolicy="no-referrer"`; each falls back to a letter tile if it fails.
- No user secrets anywhere. Server-side configuration is Redis (Upstash) credentials, the Telegram and CRON secrets, the x402 CDP keys, and the Glider tenant API key (`GLIDER_API_KEY`) — credentials for this *server*, never tied to a user or a wallet, and never sent to the browser (every `/api/baskets/*` route calls Glider from the server and resolves a user's portfolio from their address, never from a caller-supplied id). Verified via a full git-history secret scan and GitHub's native secret scanning (both clean) plus a dependency vulnerability sweep of all resolved packages (zero findings).

## Running it

```bash
npm install
npm run dev          # http://localhost:3000
```

No environment variables required to run it — the app works fully without them. Optionally:

- `KV_REST_API_URL` / `KV_REST_API_TOKEN` (or `UPSTASH_REDIS_REST_URL` / `UPSTASH_REDIS_REST_TOKEN`) — a Redis (Upstash) REST endpoint. Enables the "basis since close" sparkline's history, Telegram alerts, and the public API's key storage. Without it, the sparkline stays empty and both features return `503`.
- `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN`, `TELEGRAM_WEBHOOK_SECRET` — enables Telegram alerts. The token comes from [@BotFather](https://t.me/BotFather); the webhook secret is any random string you generate yourself, passed to Telegram's `setWebhook` as `secret_token` and checked against the `X-Telegram-Bot-Api-Secret-Token` header on every incoming update. To confirm the bot's real username, call Telegram's `getMe` with the token (do not print the token).
- `CRON_SECRET` — a random string Vercel automatically attaches as `Authorization: Bearer <secret>` on cron-triggered requests ([documented pattern](https://vercel.com/docs/cron-jobs/manage-cron-jobs#securing-cron-jobs)); secures the cron routes against being triggered by anyone else.
- `TELEGRAM_ADMIN_CHAT_ID` — maintainer-only. A daily cron diffs Coinbase's public tokenized-stock list (`api.coinbase.com/v1/tokenized-stocks`, 92 names on 2026-10-07, 58 on 2026-10-01; the older base.org/stocks page it used to scrape lists only ten) against the tickers tracked in `lib/tokens.ts`, checks each untracked one for an Aerodrome USDC pool and its real depth, and sends one Telegram digest to this chat ID (plus a ready-to-paste entry for any pool over $250k). A token is reported when it first has a pool and again when that pool first becomes liquid, never repeatedly. It never edits `lib/tokens.ts` itself — a human still reviews and adds each one. Also used to flag a real B20 `multiplier()` change (split or dividend) on any tracked stock.
- `GLIDER_API_KEY`, `GLIDER_STRATEGY_ID` (and optionally `GLIDER_API_BASE`, `BASKET_AUTOPUBLISH`) — enable Baskets; see "Baskets (Basis Tilt)". Without the first two the `/baskets` enroll panel stays off and every `/api/baskets/*` route answers `503`. `DEV_GEO_COUNTRY` (e.g. `DE`) works only under `next dev`.
- `CDP_API_KEY_ID`, `CDP_API_KEY_SECRET`, `X402_PAYOUT_ADDRESS` — enables the pay-per-call agent API (see "Pay-per-call agent API (x402)" below). The CDP keys are Coinbase Developer Platform *platform* credentials (from [portal.cdp.coinbase.com](https://portal.cdp.coinbase.com)), not a blockchain signing key — they authenticate this server to Coinbase's facilitator service, which is what actually settles payment on-chain. `X402_PAYOUT_ADDRESS` is any wallet address you control; USDC lands there directly. Without all three set, every `/api/v1/x402/*` route returns `503`.

## Public API

`/api/v1/tape` is a separate, versioned, documented contract for external consumers (bots, agents) — distinct from the unauthenticated `/api/tape` the site's own frontend polls, so internal refactors never silently break external callers.

Get a key (free, instant, no signup):
```bash
curl -X POST https://afterbook.app/api/v1/keys
```

Call the tape with it:
```bash
curl https://afterbook.app/api/v1/tape -H "Authorization: Bearer ab_..."
```

Response shape (frozen — internal-only fields like `cashTicker`/`name`/`cashStale` are deliberately not part of this contract):
```json
{
  "session": { "state": "after-hours", "label": "After-hours", "nyTime": "16:30" },
  "asOf": 1735000000000,
  "rows": [
    { "symbol": "NVDAc", "cashUsd": 218.36, "midUsd": 218.65, "basisBp": 13.5, "depthUsd": 2500000 }
  ]
}
```

Rate limit: 1 request/second per key.

## Agent / MCP

`https://afterbook.app/api/mcp` is a remote [MCP](https://modelcontextprotocol.io) server for agents (Claude, etc.) — no key, no signup, read-only. Add it as a remote MCP server in Claude Code, Claude Desktop, or any MCP-compatible client pointed at that URL.

Three tools:

- **`get_tape`** — no input. Same data as `/api/v1/tape` above.
- **`get_quote`** — `{ symbol, usdcIn }`. Shares out, execution price, and price impact for sizing a USDC → stock trade — the same estimate Lot Lab shows, not a firm quote; the actual fill always happens on Aerodrome's own app.
- **`get_basis_stats`** — `{ symbol }`. Mean/mean-absolute/max-absolute basis in bp while the cash market was closed, over the last 30 days of sampled history. `null` if there isn't enough history yet.

## Pay-per-call agent API (x402)

`/api/v1/x402/tape` and `/api/v1/x402/quote` are the same data as `/api/v1/tape` and `/api/quote` above, gated by the [x402](https://x402.org) HTTP micropayment protocol instead of an API key — $0.02 in USDC per call on Base, paid directly from an agent's own wallet via [EIP-3009](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-3009) `transferWithAuthorization`. No signup, no key, no rate-limit ceiling — payment itself is the throttle. Same $0.02 tier: **`/api/v1/x402/execute-link`** — `{ symbol, usdcIn, direction?: 'buy' | 'sell' }`, a sized trade estimate (same shape as `/api/v1/x402/quote`) plus a verified Aerodrome deep link for that exact direction. Deliberately a *link*, not raw calldata: this app has no confirmed-working router for these Slipstream pools outside Aerodrome's own frontend (their documented public SwapRouter/Quoter are bound to a different CL factory and revert on these specific pools; their Universal Router shows zero real usage against these pools in a live on-chain sample; their classic `Router.sol` is built for the old stable/volatile AMM pools, not Slipstream — all checked directly on-chain before deciding not to ship calldata construction). An agent's own execution layer opens/submits the link; this app still never constructs calldata or touches a key.

**This isn't exclusive data** — `/api/quote` and every MCP tool above stay fully free and unauthenticated, on purpose. This tier's actual value is zero setup friction for an agent that would rather pay $0.02 than provision a key and live under a rate limit.

Two higher, $0.05 endpoints for agents doing heavier analysis:

- **`/api/v1/x402/history`** — `{ symbol }`. 30 days of sampled basis history (same data `/api/history` serves free) plus the pool's current on-chain tick-liquidity distribution (same data `/api/depth` serves free) — bundled into one call for backtesting agents that want both the historical mispricing signal and how thick the book was at the time.
- **`/api/v1/x402/lp-range`** — `{ symbol, holdingDays?, targetProbabilityPct? }`. A recommended concentrated-liquidity tick range sized from realized volatility over a target holding period (default 7 days, 70% coverage), widened when the symbol's next earnings date falls inside that window. This one has no free equivalent — it's genuinely new derived output, not a convenience bundle of already-free routes. The earnings widening is a documented flat heuristic (no options-implied-vol data source exists in this app), not a real implied move. Also returns `divergenceLossAtBoundary` — the impermanent-loss benchmark at each edge of that range (`lib/lpRange.ts`).

**Afterbook never holds a signing key for this.** Settlement is executed by Coinbase's hosted CDP facilitator, not by this app — Afterbook only supplies a plain receiving wallet address (`X402_PAYOUT_ADDRESS`) and a CDP *platform* API key/secret pair that authenticates this server to Coinbase's facilitator service (not a blockchain key). Payment lands directly in that address; nothing is ever custodied here, same as everywhere else in this app. Proceeds are not automatically swapped or spent — that's a deliberately separate, not-yet-built piece.

All five routes 503 with `{ "error": "x402 tier not configured" }` until `CDP_API_KEY_ID`, `CDP_API_KEY_SECRET`, and `X402_PAYOUT_ADDRESS` are all set.
