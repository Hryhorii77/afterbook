# Afterbook

**Live at [afterbook.app](https://afterbook.app).**

Cash close vs the Aero book, in shares, for the Coinbase tokenized stocks on Base. The tape, Lot Lab and My Lots are read-only — no wallet ever signs anything there. Two places ask your wallet to sign, both non-custodial: **Buy / Sell** in the Execute panel (an in-app swap from your own wallet through KyberSwap's router, see "In-app swaps" below) and **Baskets** (`/baskets`), run on [Glider](https://glider.fi), where *you* sign an enrollment and withdrawals and send USDC from your own wallet. See "Baskets (Basis Tilt)" below.

**Pages:** `/` and `/[symbol]` (the tape for a stock), `/today` ("Biggest gap"), `/borrow`, `/build` (basket builder), `/baskets`, `/baskets/risks`.

## What it shows

- **Tape**: cash-market last price vs Aerodrome's on-chain mid price for the twelve Coinbase tokenized stocks Afterbook tracks (NVDAc, AAPLc, METAc, GOOGLc, AMZNc, MSFTc, MSTRc, SNDKc, SPCXc, TSLAc, plus PLTRc and MUc added 2026-10-01), basis in bp, plus real pool depth. Each row is a card with the token's icon, a 24h change and a past-N-days chart (see "UI architecture"). During pre-market and after-hours, "cash" is the live extended-hours print (Yahoo's `preMarketPrice`/`postMarketPrice`), not the frozen 4pm close — comparing on-chain against a stale close would misreport a real overnight tradfi move as basis. While cash is closed, the hero's gap number and sentence (and `/today`) stay anchored to the actual regular-session close, not the live extended-hours print, so the home page and `/today` never disagree about the same instant. In pre-market/after-hours the hero labels the cash price by the row's real `cashPriceType`: only when Yahoo has an extended-hours print does it say "cash pre-market/after-hours price"; with no print the cash price is still the last close, so it says "Friday's close" and the card says "Cash close". The tape's cash column header and the note under "Tape" follow the same rule, computed from all rows: no row on a print → "Cash close" and "No pre-market trades yet, so the basis is measured against the last regular-session close"; every row on a print → "Cash pre-market/after-hours"; a mix → "Cash (latest)" and a note saying it is a live print where there is one and the last close otherwise. Link-preview, share-card and footer copy use plain language, not the "Aero book" jargon. A symbol reporting earnings within 5 days gets a small "Earnings in Nd" flag in the tape and a caveat in Lot Lab — a widened spread near earnings is expected, not a sign the tape's basis math is broken.
- **Liquid vs thin split**: pools with real depth (≥ $1M; at the time of writing NVDAc/AAPLc/METAc/GOOGLc/AMZNc/MSTRc/SNDKc/SPCXc — MSFTc, TSLAc, PLTRc and MUc are thin) lead the tape and hero grid; everything thinner collapses behind a "Show N thin books" toggle at reduced size. A $10k pool's basis swings hundreds of bp on noise alone — giving it the same visual weight as a $2M pool made the tape read as broken. $100k used to be the bar, but `depthUsd` is a pool's total real token balance, not liquidity active near the current tick — a pool can clear $100k on stale/off-range positions while still swinging hundreds of bp (MSTRc did, at ~$965k depth and +257bp), so the bar moved to where the pools actually separate into a calm tier and a still-noisy one. The split is driven by live depth, not a hardcoded symbol list, so a thin pool graduates automatically once it actually has liquidity. Landing directly on a thin symbol (URL or dropdown) gets a small "thin book · $Xk depth" chip under the sticky bar, since the hero/tape's own thin-tier demotion is otherwise the only clue.
- **Every tokenized stock (`/stocks`)**: all of Coinbase's tokenized stocks (173 on 2026-10-18), searchable and filterable, each with its icon, contract (BaseScan link) and Aerodrome USDC pool status: **Tracked** (the `STOCKS` list, linking to its page, with the tape's live depth), **Small pool** (an Aerodrome USDC pool too shallow to track, with its depth; 46 on 2026-10-18, deepest LLYc at $231k), **No pool yet**, or **Not checked yet** (listed by Coinbase after the last daily check). Untracked tokens get no gap, no alerts and no swaps: in a pool that small one trade moves the price, so the gap is noise. The pool column comes from the daily `discover-tokens` job, which already measures every untracked token and saves one snapshot (`directory:pools`, `saveDirectoryPools` in `lib/stockDirectory.ts`); the page reads it through Next's data cache (an hour) and Coinbase's list (cached an hour), so a page view costs no chain reads for untracked tokens and at most one Redis request an hour. Linked under the tape table and listed in the sitemap; not in the header nav, which is full on phones.
- **Memestocks (`/memes`, `/memes/<address>`)**: Base meme tokens whose pool is quoted in one of the tracked stock tokens (BLUECHIP/NVDAc, IPOD/AAPLc…), each with its 1h/6h/24h move next to its stock's, price, 24h volume, liquidity, the stock held in the pool, market cap and pool age. A not-dismissable warning sits above the numbers ("Most memecoins go to zero; the stock token in the pool does not make the meme safer"), the list is sorted by volume or by move, never by our pick, and pools under $10k of liquidity sit under "thin". **The list is its own allowlist** (`memes:pools` in Redis, `lib/memestocks.ts`), written by the daily `discover-memes` job; it never touches `STOCKS` or `BASKET_SYMBOLS`. The job reads Dexscreener's pairs for every tracked stock, drops majors and stablecoins (by address, and any symbol containing USD/EUR) and other Coinbase stock tokens, and lists a pool only if it is proven on the chain: Aerodrome Slipstream and Uniswap v3 through their factories' `getPool`; Uniswap v4 (most of this market, 2026-10-18) through PositionManager's `poolKeys` hashing to the pool id ("proven"), or, for pools created outside PositionManager (launchpads), the pool existing in StateView with liquidity and a price within 3% of the pair ("price-matched"; public RPCs refuse the old logs that would prove the key). A meme must also have a KyberSwap route back to USDC (a basic can-it-be-sold check). Memes are identified by contract address: several tokens share names (three "GPU"s on 2026-10-18). Live numbers come from Dexscreener (cached 2 minutes) and the allowlist through Next's data cache (an hour), so a view costs no chain reads. Each meme page has its own share card (`/card?page=meme&addr=…`).
- **Hero card + compact bar**: the first thing on the tape page. It opens on the day's biggest liquid gap (the same pick `/today` uses, chosen on the server in `app/page.tsx`; `/NVDAc` etc. still open on that stock). It holds the stock chooser, the market-session pill, the big gap number with one plain sentence ("Strategy (MSTR) is trading 2.7% (268.2 bp) above Friday's close on Aerodrome. Cash reopens Mon 9:30 AM ET.": percent first, because strangers read it faster, bp in brackets; under 5 bp only the bp is shown, since the percent would round to 0.00% and look like a mistake: `gapSizeText` in `lib/format.ts`, also used for the share text), cash and Aero price cards, a primary "Size a SYMBOL trade" button (scrolls to Lot Lab, where the eligibility check lives; it never opens Aerodrome directly), a Telegram alerts link (to `t.me/afterbook_bot`; the bot's display name is "AfterbookAlertsBot", which is not its username), and a one-time note defining Aero and "100 bp = 1%". While cash is closed the number is anchored to the actual close (same as `/today`), not a live extended-hours print. A compact bar with the same number sticks to the top once the hero scrolls away. The page's `<h1>` is visually hidden.
- **No tile grid:** an earlier version repeated every liquid stock as a clickable tile under the hero (the same numbers as the ticker and the table). It was removed; the tape rows below are the way to switch stock, and clicking one still jumps to Lot Lab.
- **Basis since close**: a sparkline of the selected symbol's basis from the last cash close to now, sampled into Redis every 5 minutes by the alerts cron (`recordTapeSamples` in `lib/tape.ts`, one write per stock per tick however many serverless instances are warm; until 2026-10-18 each instance sampled off its own traffic, which helped use up the Upstash plan's monthly request cap), and it works with zero setup (just renders empty) if no Redis store is configured. Recorded for every tracked stock regardless of the liquid/thin split above — it briefly reused that same $1M gate and silently stopped recording six symbols' history until that got decoupled; a thin pool's basis history is at least as useful to someone deciding whether to trade it. The same 30-day retained history also powers two one-line stats underneath, once there's enough sampled history: mean/max basis while the cash market was closed, and an **open-snap** convergence stat (`getOpenSnapStats`, `lib/history.ts`) — for each trading day, compares the last sample before that day's 9:30am NY open against samples ~30/90 min after, and reports how often |basis| shrank rather than widened ("basis moved toward $0 within 30 min in X% of the last N sessions"). Requires at least 8 valid trading days in the window before showing anything, same "collecting data" honesty as the sparkline itself rather than a percentage computed off too few days.
- **NY session clock**: cash-open / pre-market / after-hours / weekend / holiday state, so the 24/7-onchain-vs-24/5-cash gap is explicit, not implied.
- **Lot Lab**: type a USDC amount (or pick a size preset — $250 / $1k / $5k / 1% of the pool), see shares out, execution price, and a price-impact curve across a log-spaced range of trade sizes ($500–$1M) — computed from the pool's live `slot0()`/`liquidity()`, not a cached reserve snapshot. The panel is split into **Trade / LP / Carry** tabs (Trade is the default) rather than stacking trade sizing, the depth chart, and the carry math into one long card — keeps the primary execution flow front and center and makes the tool readable on mobile. While cash is closed and the basis spread exceeds 20bp, also shows a cash-and-carry edge calculator (`lib/arb.ts`) in its own Carry tab: gross basis, pool fee, price impact, and a flat gas estimate netted out, then annualized over the actual time remaining until the cash market reopens — the tab itself is hidden outside that window rather than shown with nothing worth acting on. Still buy-only in the sense that this app never shorts or borrows, but the Carry tab now has both directions: a **buy basis** side (on-chain priced *below* cash — the classic setup) and a **short basis / inventory-unwind** side (on-chain priced *above* cash — for someone who already holds spot shares and wants to sell into that premium now, then rebuy the same notional in cash pre-market once it reopens). `computeCashAndCarryEdge` (`lib/arb.ts`) takes a `direction` param that mirrors the sign; the toggle defaults to whichever side actually has an edge for the active symbol's current basis. A **delta-neutral hedge** note below the calculator explains pairing either side with a same-notional perp position to strip out the stock's own price risk — links to real, individually-verified venues (Hyperliquid, Avantis, Synthetix) for checking live funding, not a live integration this app computes itself (no third-party price/data API, on purpose — see "Why the math is done the way it is"). Also shows the same amount priced as a full-range LP position (≈50/50 by value — exact for full-range concentrated liquidity, with a caveat that Aerodrome defaults new deposits to a narrower range). Below that, a liquidity-depth chart (`lib/depth.ts`) reconstructs active liquidity across a ±25% price window from the pool's own tick-bitmap/`ticks()` data — no subgraph, no indexer. Slipstream's `ticks()` returns 10 fields, not upstream V3's 8; until 2026-10-12 it was decoded with the V3 layout, every read failed, and the chart showed the current tick's liquidity flat across the window. The fix was checked by summing the USDC the reconstruction puts below the price against each pool's real USDC balance (within ~2% for all twelve). The chart doubles as an interactive LP range selector: drag its two handles to size a candidate range and see, live, its capital efficiency (`lib/lpRange.ts` — derived from the same virtual-reserve equations as the impact curve, not a recalled formula), an estimated fee APR for the range (the APR a full-range unstaked position earned, read from the pool's own `feeGrowthGlobal` counters, times that multiplier; `rangeAprPct` in `lib/lpRange.ts`), the probability the range is still in range at the same 7-day horizon My Lots uses for real positions, and a **divergence-loss-at-boundary** figure (`computeDivergenceLossAtBoundary`, `lib/lpRange.ts`) — the standard concentrated-liquidity impermanent-loss benchmark if price reaches exactly the low or high edge, verified against the classic Uniswap V2 IL formula in the full-range limit before trusting it. Dragging itself is gated behind an explicit "Adjust range" toggle, off by default — the range stays visible either way, but a finger landing on the chart on mobile scrolls the page instead of getting captured as a drag unless that toggle is on. Handles clamp to each other and to the current price, so a dragged range always brackets it — the underlying math assumes that. Below the range metrics, an **auto-recenter** note explains what re-centering an out-of-range position actually involves and links to Gelato Web3 Functions and Bankr for building that automation yourself — Afterbook doesn't run it, for the same reason it doesn't construct swap calldata (see "Why the math is done the way it is" above). A "Copy trade" button copies the sized trade as plain text. When a symbol has real earnings-move history (see below), Lot Lab also shows an **earnings volatility spread**: how many days of the stock's realized volatility the pool's own liquidity concentration (`lib/lpRange.ts`'s highest-density-region construction) would justify, compared to the actual days until the next report and the historical average size of this stock's post-earnings moves (`lib/earningsHistory.ts`). Not implied volatility in the options-market sense — there's no options market on these pools — a liquidity-concentration proxy translated into a comparable "days" unit via the same realized-vol model used elsewhere, not a fabricated number.
- **My Lots**: a wallet chip, summary cards (total / spot / Aerodrome LP value) and one card per holding (spot as icon, USD value and share of holdings; LP with an in-range badge and range bar). Connect a wallet — a RainbowKit picker showing every installed browser-extension wallet (Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, etc., detected live via EIP-6963) plus a manual fallback, read-only — the connection only reveals your public address, nothing is ever signed — to see spot share balances and Aerodrome LP positions across the tracked stocks, priced live. Shows your [Basename](https://www.base.org/names) instead of the raw address when one's set, resolved directly against Base's own L2Resolver — no separate mainnet RPC, no extra dependency. LP position data comes from Aerodrome/Velodrome's own "Sugar" read-helper contract (`positionsByFactory`), not the publicly-documented NFT position manager — that one is bound to a different CL factory than these specific pools use and would silently show nothing. Each LP position also shows its price range with in-range/out-of-range status, fees and AERO emissions earned, and lock expiry if locked — all fields Sugar already returns on the same call, just surfaced rather than discarded. The range is now also a visual bar marking where the current price sits, plus two estimates: fee APR for the position's own range while it is in range (from daily on-chain snapshots of the pool's own `feeGrowthGlobal0X128`/`feeGrowthGlobal1X128` counters, which are fees per unit of unstaked in-range liquidity, already net of Slipstream's 10% `unstakedFee` cut; turned into a full-range APR and multiplied by the range's capital efficiency, not a third-party volume API). Until 2026-10-12 this was "fees x one snapshot's active liquidity / pool value", which swung with whatever liquidity the snapshot caught, counted staked liquidity (up to 96% of a pool) that earns no fees, and then got multiplied by capital efficiency on top of already-concentrated LPs: Lot Lab showed 6,093% for an NVDAc range that earns about 12% and the odds the price is still in range 7 days out (from realized volatility of recently-sampled on-chain price, via a standard lognormal estimate). Both are on-chain-only by design — no third-party price/volume API. In-range odds still start as "collecting data" until enough samples exist, but fee APR no longer does: `computeFeeAprWithFallback` (`lib/feeApr.ts`) falls back to summing real Swap-event volume over the most recent ~1900 blocks (confirmed live: mainnet.base.org caps `eth_getLogs` at a 2,000-block range) and annualizing that window's fee per unit of liquidity (fee on each swap's input, less the unstaked cut, over the swap's liquidity), whenever there isn't yet enough feeGrowthGlobal snapshot history — verified end-to-end against real chain data for three live pools before trusting it. Labeled "prelim." in the UI so it doesn't read as the same-confidence real figure. My Lots also shows a **gauge-vs-fee yield comparison** (`lib/gaugeYield.ts`): the AERO staking APR for the same range (`rewardRate` per unit of staked liquidity, on the same full-range-times-capital-efficiency basis as the fee APR) next to the unstaked fee APR above, so the panel says outright which currently earns more. Out-of-range positions say they earn neither. AERO's own USD price is read from a real on-chain Aerodrome pool (found via the CL factory's `getPool`, not hardcoded) — this app previously had no AERO price source at all and showed emissions as a raw token amount for exactly that reason; that gap's closed now. Staked liquidity's USD value can't use the same virtual-reserve math the impact curve does (that describes trade-impact behavior, not actual holdings, and overstates a concentrated position's real value) — instead it scales the pool's real, balance-based depth by the staked/total liquidity ratio, an approximation that assumes staked and unstaked positions are concentrated similarly on average. A position also gets flagged when the most recent epoch's veAERO voting incentives (bribes, from Aerodrome's `RewardsSugar.epochsByAddress`) outpaid real trading fees for that same pool/epoch — a signal that a pool's gauge APR may be governance-subsidized rather than earned from organic volume.
- **Visitor counts** (`app/layout.tsx`): Vercel Web Analytics (`@vercel/analytics`, the `<Analytics />` tag, switched on in the Vercel project's Analytics tab). It is cookieless, stores no personal data and counts page views and visitors per page and referrer; it knows nothing about wallets. The script is served from the site's own `/_vercel/insights/` path, so the CSP needed no change (`'strict-dynamic'` covers it), and it 404s under `next start`/`next dev` locally, which is expected: only the live site can confirm it. Outbound clicks are logged separately (`lib/clicks.ts`).
- **Deposit visibility** (`BasketPanel.tsx`): the USDC held in the user's Basis Tilt smart account is read straight from the chain every 15 seconds (`balanceOf` on the deposit address), so a deposit is visible as soon as it confirms. If Glider's positions still say $0.00 the panel explains that the money is the user's, only they can withdraw it, and Withdraw stays off until Glider counts it; after a send it links the transaction on Basescan. Measured 2026-10-08: a 2 USDC deposit showed on-chain at once and Glider counted it in about an hour (not at the daily run, whose `nextDueAt` is shown by the API but is not what triggers the count).
- **Base Builder Code** (ERC-8021, `lib/builderCode.ts`): `bc_hebg61t4`, registered at base.dev, is appended to the calldata of every transaction the app asks a wallet to send, so Base can attribute that activity to Afterbook (the dashboard is Settings → Project Settings → Builder Code; it tracks **transactions**, not website visitors). The app sends three kinds: the USDC deposit on the Baskets panel, and the exact-amount approval and the swap in the Execute panel's Buy / Sell and in a `/build` basket buy ("In-app swaps"). Liquidity still happens on Aerodrome's own site and cannot carry the code. It is passed per call as `dataSuffix` (the swap, sent with `sendTransaction`, has it appended to its calldata with `concat`): a code set in `createConfig` is ignored by `useWriteContract` (wevm/wagmi #5248, open in 2026-10). Check any transaction by its calldata ending in `80218021802180218021802180218021`.
- **Wallets on phones** (2026-10-05): a phone browser has no extensions, so the RainbowKit list was useless there (before `NEXT_PUBLIC_WALLETCONNECT_PROJECT_ID` was set in Vercel Production it was just a dead "Browser Wallet" and Coinbase). Now, on a phone in a normal browser (no injected provider), **"Connect wallet" opens WalletConnect's own "All Wallets" window directly** (`WalletConnectButton.tsx` calls the connector RainbowKit builds with `showQrModal`): a search box over hundreds of wallets, with MetaMask, Rabby, Trust, Rainbow, OKX and Zerion listed first (`explorerRecommendedWalletIds` in `lib/wagmiConfig.ts`; the ids come from WalletConnect's registry: `curl 'https://api.web3modal.org/getWallets?projectId=<id>&st=appkit&sv=html-core-1.7.8&page=1&entries=5&search=<name>&chains=eip155%3A8453'`, with curl, not Python urllib which gets a 403); pick one, tap Open, the wallet app opens, approves and returns, as on O1 exchange. The RainbowKit "Connect a Wallet" list with its "What is a Wallet?" box no longer appears there. Desktop, and a phone inside a wallet app's own browser (injected provider present), keep the RainbowKit list; `modalSize="compact"` removes the "What is a Wallet?" panel on desktop (a small "New to Ethereum wallets? Learn More" footer remains). MetaMask is not in the RainbowKit list: its entry uses MetaMask's own SDK relay and did nothing when tapped on a phone, while MetaMask works through WalletConnect's list; on desktop it also clashes with Rabby (see below). The project id is public (inlined in the browser bundle, so env changes need a redeploy) and `afterbook.app` is on the Reown Cloud project's allowlist. The CSP (`middleware.ts`) allows `blob:` images (the wallet icons) and `verify.walletconnect.org/.com` frames; Google Fonts for that window stay blocked on purpose (system fonts are used).
- **Wallet connect mechanics**: `app/providers.tsx`/`lib/wagmiConfig.ts` (wagmi + RainbowKit), with a second always-visible "Connect wallet" entry point in the header (`app/components/WalletConnectButton.tsx`) alongside My Lots' own — both read the same global wagmi account state, so connecting or disconnecting from either stays in sync. Disconnect calls `wallet_revokePermissions` (MetaMask's real revoke) before clearing local state — clearing local state alone leaves the wallet extension still considering the site authorized, so the next Connect would silently reuse the same account with no way to switch. No `metaMaskWallet` entry, deliberately: RainbowKit's per-wallet detection is flag-based (`window.ethereum.isMetaMask`), not EIP-6963/rdns, and several alternative wallets (Rabby included) set that same compatibility flag for dapp-compat purposes — with both installed, whichever wallet currently holds `window.ethereum` answers to "MetaMask" regardless of which one is real, and confirmed in practice (`Error: MetaMask extension not found`, thrown from inside the click) rather than just connecting to the wrong wallet silently. `rabbyWallet`'s own entry checks `isRabby` specifically, which only Rabby sets, so it doesn't have this ambiguity; the generic "Browser Wallet" entry remains the correct fallback for a real MetaMask-only setup with no other flag-setting wallet installed. WalletConnect/mobile-wallet options (and Trust/OKX/Zerion/Rainbow, whose desktop connectors fall back to WalletConnect when their own extension isn't installed) only appear when `NEXT_PUBLIC_WALLETCONNECT_PROJECT_ID` (a free [Reown Cloud](https://cloud.reown.com) project id) is set — without one, that connector eagerly opens a relay socket at page load and, observed directly, spins a Chrome renderer's CPU into a runaway climb rather than failing quietly, so it's left out entirely rather than shipped half-working. Every extension-based wallet (Coinbase Wallet, Rabby, plus any other EIP-6963-announced extension via the generic "Browser Wallet" entry) works with no env var at all. The `base`/"Base Account" wallet RainbowKit ships by default is deliberately excluded from the wallet list (`lib/wagmiConfig.ts`) — it transitively imports `@coinbase/cdp-sdk`'s Solana code path, which this EVM-only app doesn't have installed, and breaks the production build outright.
- **Execute**: **Buy / Sell** the stock in-app from the connected wallet (`SwapPanel.tsx`, see "In-app swaps"), plus Aerodrome's own swap and add-liquidity UIs under "Or trade on Aerodrome's own site", all gated behind a non-US geo check and an eligibility checkbox. `/today` links to the biggest gap's Execute panel (`/<SYMBOL>#execute`). A one-line trade summary ("AMZNc · $2,500 · ~9.67 sh · pool 0xd03B…") sits above the eligibility checkbox, visible regardless of whether it's checked — a user shouldn't have to affirm a legal checkbox just to see what they'd be trading — with its own "Copy lot" button that copies the whole line as plain text (`AMZNc $2500 ~9.67 0xd03Bc8...`, full untruncated pool address) for pasting into a chat/ticket/agent prompt. Aerodrome's swap URL only accepts `from`/`to`/`chain` params — no amount (verified live: typing into the Sell field updates the page but never the URL, and an `&amount=` param is silently ignored) — so inside "Or trade on Aerodrome's own site" a "Copy $X" button copies just the bare numeric amount for Aerodrome's own amount field, and a 3-step checklist (copy → open → paste and sign) spells out the sequence. Those Aerodrome links are secondary buttons now; the panel's one primary action is the swap's Review / Buy. Below the token/pool addresses, a **backing & issuer** note shows the token's live `totalSupply()` (real, on-chain, confirmed via a direct contract read) next to the custodian claim (Alpaca Securities, ADGM-supervised, 1:1, per Coinbase's own published docs) — explicitly labeled as the issuer's stated claim, not something this app verified, since there's no on-chain reserve-attestation contract for B20 tokens the way there is for some wrapped assets (checked directly: `owner()`/`paused()` both revert on the token contract, so there's no standard admin/pause interface to read either).
- **Log**: a session-local, timestamped feed of notable events (starting with "cash just closed" plus each liquid symbol's basis at that instant).
- **Telegram alerts**: `/alert STOCK BP` (`NVDA` or `NVDAc`, any case) pings when a stock's |basis| crosses a threshold, then re-arms only once the gap is back to 80% of the level or lower (`REARM_RATIO` and `nextBasisState` in `lib/alerts.ts`; before 2026-10-08 it re-armed the instant the gap touched the line and a gap hovering near 50 bp sent about 14 messages in a day and a half, visible in a user's public screenshot), and a missing tape reading keeps the state so a data blip can't re-arm it; `/close` pings once with every liquid name's basis the moment the cash market closes.
  - **Loan alerts** (since 2026-10-14): `/watch 0xADDRESS [HEALTH]` watches a wallet's stock-backed USDC loans on Base, Aave V4's MAG7 spoke and every listed Morpho stock market (`lib/loanPositions.ts`, on-chain reads; Morpho Blue `0xBBBB…EFFCb`).
    - **When it warns:** once, when the lowest health drops below the threshold (default 1.2, allowed 1.01–3; liquidation is below 1.00). It re-arms only above threshold × 1.05 (`nextLoanState`, `LOAN_REARM_FACTOR` in `lib/loanHealth.ts`).
    - **Aerodrome's price:** health is also worked out at Aerodrome's price, the edge here. Lending oracles follow the shut cash market while Aerodrome trades, so the warning can come before the oracle moves, and the message says when that is the reason.
    - **Failed reads and closed loans:** a failed read keeps the state, so it can't fire or re-arm. A wallet with no loans re-arms.
    - **Limits:** one wallet per chat, the free tier (`MAX_LOAN_RULES_PER_CHAT`); a second `/watch` replaces the first. More wallets and custom thresholds are the planned paid tier.
    - **`/loans`** shows the watched wallet's loans now.
    - **Storage:** the address is stored with the chat's rules in Redis until `/stop`. Addresses are public, and the bot never asks anyone to connect a wallet or sign anything.
    - **Checked against the sources:** health matches Aave's `getUserAccountData` and Morpho's API to 4 decimals (checked 2026-10-14 on real borrowers).
  - A Vercel Cron job re-evaluates every 5 minutes against live tape data and fires only on the transition, not every tick a condition holds. The bot is **@afterbook_bot** (`t.me/afterbook_bot`, linked from the tape hero); its display name, "AfterbookAlertsBot", is *not* a username and `t.me/AfterbookAlertsBot` does not exist.
- **Live ticker strip** under the header on every page: each stock's on-chain price with a coloured ▲/▼ for its live basis, as a slow marquee. Each item links to that stock's page (`/NVDAc` etc.). It pauses while a mouse hovers it (only on devices that really hover — see the note in `globals.css`), runs again on leave and right after a tap, and never pauses on focus or press. `prefers-reduced-motion` stops it.
- **Biggest gap** (`/today`): the single biggest cash-vs-chain gap right now as a hero card (icon, gap, cash and Aero prices, and what it takes to trade it: price impact of a $25k clip and the size needed to move the price 50/100 bp), liquidity-behind-the-tape stat cards, and a "who mean-reverts at the open" card per stock. - **Borrow** (`/borrow`): every USDC lending market on Base that takes a tracked stock as collateral, from two lenders, in one shape (`lib/lendingMarket.ts`):

## Why the math is done the way it is

The stock/USDC pools are Aerodrome **Slipstream** (concentrated-liquidity, Uniswap-V3-style) pools, not classic constant-product pools — confirmed on-chain (`stable()`/`getReserves()` revert, `slot0()`/`liquidity()`/`tickSpacing()` work). Aerodrome's documented public Quoter and SwapRouter are bound to a *different* CL factory than the one these pools were deployed through (`pool.factory() != quoter.factory()`, verified on-chain), so the standard Quoter reverts on these pools and can't be used here.

Instead, `lib/quote.ts` reads `slot0()` and `liquidity()` directly and uses the fact that a CL pool behaves exactly like a constant-product pool with virtual reserves `(L / sqrtP, L * sqrtP)` as long as a trade stays within the current tick's liquidity — that's exact math, not an approximation, but it stops being exact once a trade is large enough to cross into a different tick range (flagged as `largeTradeCaveat`, and shaded on the impact curve). **Since 2026-10-12 every served estimate walks the real tick liquidity instead** (`lib/lotQuote.ts` → `simulateBuy` in `lib/swapSim.ts`, over the ±25% window `lib/depth.ts` reads): Lot Lab, `/api/quote`, the MCP `get_quote` tool, the x402 quote and execute-link routes and `/today`'s sizing cards. Single-tick math was off in both directions past the current tick (NVDAc $100k: 8.2 bp single-tick vs 14.5 bp walked; SPCXc $100k: 45 vs 39), with no caveat shown. `largeTradeCaveat` keeps its name but now means the trade ran past that window; the cost shown is then for the part that fits in it, a lower bound the UI prefixes with "≥" (the first deploy divided the full amount by only the fillable shares and showed TSLAc $500k as +30,226 bp; fixed the same day). The single-tick `estimateLot` remains only as the fallback if the tick scan fails. The x402 execute-link route with `direction=sell` used to return the *buy* estimate; it now sizes a sell by its dollar value at the Aero price and returns `sharesIn`/`usdcOut` from the sell-side walk. This is explicitly framed in the UI as an estimate for sizing a trade, not a firm quote — the actual fill always happens on Aerodrome's own app.

Pool depth (the tape's Depth column, and the liquid/thin split) comes from real `token.balanceOf(pool)` reads — deliberately *not* the virtual reserves used for impact math, which describe in-range liquidity depth and run much larger than what's actually deployed.

All token and pool addresses in `lib/tokens.ts` were verified on-chain (`decimals()`, `token0()`/`token1()`, `fee()`, `factory()`) and cross-checked against Dexscreener's Aerodrome pair listings — the original four on 2026-09-04, plus AMZNc/MSFTc/MSTRc/SNDKc/SPCXc/TSLAc on 2026-09-05 when Base expanded to ten tokenized stocks. All ten share the same CL factory, 0.05% fee tier, and tick spacing. PLTRc and MUc were added on 2026-10-01 after being matched against Coinbase's own public list (`api.coinbase.com/v1/tokenized-stocks`: contract address, decimals, `multiplier()`), checked on-chain (USDC is `token0`, fee 500, tick spacing 10) and against Dexscreener's Aerodrome listings; both pools hold roughly $0.6M, so they sit in the thin tier. Liquidity on the newer six is much thinner ($10k–$110k vs $1–2M on the original four) — that's why the tape splits them out, and why their impact curves and `largeTradeCaveat` trip at far smaller sizes.

Each stock token also implements the [B20 asset standard](https://docs.base.org/get-started/issue-rwa)'s `multiplier()` — a fixed-point scalar the issuer can change without migrating raw balances. This isn't split-only, despite how this file used to describe it: per Base's own B20 spec, cash dividends are reinvested into additional shares by raising the same multiplier, net of withholding tax and a Coinbase fee. All tracked tokens read 1.0x today, but `lib/quote.ts` reads it live on every quote rather than assuming 1.0 forever, so "shares out" stays correct the moment either happens — and `lib/corporateActions.ts` watches for exactly that: a Telegram ping to the maintainer whenever a symbol's multiplier changes from its last known value, since that's a real corporate action worth a human glance. The active stock's page also shows the redemption ratio directly whenever it's not 1.0x.

On-chain reads go through `https://mainnet.base.org`, Base's documented official RPC endpoint, with a public third-party RPC as a fallback.

Earnings dates come from Nasdaq's public, undocumented `api.nasdaq.com/api/calendar/earnings` endpoint — no key, same "open but unofficial" trust shape as the Yahoo endpoint above. Yahoo's own calendar endpoint (`quoteSummary` + `calendarEvents`, which used to return this) now needs a crumb+cookie handshake this app doesn't have, so it wasn't an option. A ticker with no date on file (expect this for SPCX, which only IPO'd in mid-2026) just shows nothing, not an error.

`lib/earningsHistory.ts` separately accumulates *past* reports (the calendar endpoint above only ever holds the next upcoming date, overwritten as time passes) — for each report, the % move from the last close before it to the first close on or after the day after, from Yahoo's historical daily-close data (same host, a longer `period1`/`period2` window instead of `range=1d`). Seeded with a one-time ~400-day backfill; the daily earnings-sync cron extends it by a few days' lag going forward, so the sample grows every quarter. A symbol with no reports yet in the window just shows no historical-average stat, not a fabricated one.

## In-app swaps

Buy / Sell in the Execute panel swaps USDC ↔ a tracked stock token from the visitor's own wallet. The trade is routed by [KyberSwap](https://kyberswap.com)'s aggregator, which splits it across Aerodrome and other Base pools (prop AMMs such as Metric, Bebop, Kipseli); in the spike on 2026-10-13 it routed all twelve tokens at $1k and $10k within −5 to +8 bp of the Aerodrome-only quote. KyberSwap was chosen over 0x because it needs no account or API key (no new secret), takes no protocol fee on swaps (it keeps positive slippage), and supports an integrator fee.

- **Flow:** `/api/swap/quote` (price preview, refreshed every 15 s) → Review → `/api/swap/build` builds the transaction for the connected wallet and checks it (`checkSwapCalldata` in `lib/swap.ts`) → the review card shows pay, estimate, minimum received, Afterbook fee, route and router → Confirm: an exact-amount `approve` of the router for the reviewed amount if the allowance is short; after an approval, or if the card has been open over 60 s, a fresh rebuild that is sent only if it is the same trade with a minimum at least as good as the card's (otherwise the card shows the new numbers and asks again); then the swap with the Builder Code appended → `/api/swap/log`. The wallet never signs a minimum lower than the one on screen.
- **Contract:** KyberSwap `MetaAggregationRouterV2` at `0x6131B5fae19EA4f9D964eAc0408E4408b66337b5` (verified on Sourcify). The only contract a swap calls or an approval names.
- **Limits:** 0.5% slippage (the router reverts below the minimum), $25,000 per swap, 30 swap API calls per minute per IP (`swapLimiter`; KyberSwap's public limit is 3 requests/s for the whole server).
- **Fee:** `SWAP_FEE_BPS` (1–100) to `SWAP_FEE_RECIPIENT`, both required, else no fee. Always taken in USDC: on the way in for a buy, out for a sell. The review card shows it in % and dollars. Owner decision: the fee stays at 0 until a legal check on charging a fee on tokenized-stock trades.
- **Gates:** same as the rest of Execute: the non-US region check (fail-closed, on every `/api/swap/*` route too) and the eligibility checkbox.
- **Log and stats:** `/api/swap/build` remembers keccak256 of every calldata it returns (2 hours, with the account and fee); `/api/swap/log` re-reads the hash from the chain and records it only if the mined calldata (minus the Builder Code) is one of those, it succeeded, and it was sent by that account. Fee and size come from the stored build (size capped at $25,000), so a hand-made router call with the public Builder Code cannot inflate the numbers. Wallets are stored as a short pseudonymous hash (counts unique traders; not a privacy guarantee). `/api/swap/stats?days=7` (cron bearer, like `/api/clicks/stats`) returns swaps, unique traders, volume, fees and per-stock counts.
- **Basket buys** (`/build/<spec>`, "Buy it", since 2026-10-14; `BasketBuyPanel.tsx`, `lib/basketBuy.ts`):
  - **The split:** one USDC amount is split by the basket's weights (`splitBasketUsdc`: floors each leg, gives the remainder to the biggest, always sums exactly).
  - **The review:** each leg is built and checked by `/api/swap/build` like a single buy. The review card shows every leg's estimate and an "at least" amount, which is the leg's minimum less `BASKET_DRIFT_BPS` (0.5%).
  - **Signing:** one approval of exactly the total for the router (skipped if the allowance already covers it), then one swap per stock, each rebuilt just before signing. The panel pauses 4 s after each confirmed leg (`LEG_PAUSE_MS`), because on the first real basket (AMZNc + MSTRc, 2026-10-14) Rabby showed a false `TRANSFER_FROM_FAILED` on the second leg: its simulation counted the first swap twice. The same swap simulated fine on-chain and went through on "Review the rest". A leg is sent only if its minimum is at least the card's "at least" figure, so the wallet never signs below what was shown.
  - **When it stops:** a decline, a revert or a price move halts the run. The panel lists what was bought and offers "Review the rest" for the remaining legs, and nothing retries on its own. It also says the router may keep an approval for the unbought USDC.
  - **Limits:** at least $1 per leg, $25,000 per basket.
  - **Selling** ("Sell basket", `BasketSellPanel.tsx`, `basketSellAmounts`):
    - **How much:** a whole percent (1–100) of whatever the wallet holds of each of the basket's stocks, including any bought outside the basket (the panel lists the holdings and says so). Stocks the wallet doesn't hold are left out.
    - **Signing:** each stock is a different token, so each leg asks for its own approval of exactly what it sells, for the router only (skipped if the allowance covers it), then its swap to USDC. That is up to 2 requests per stock, with the same 4 s pause after each.
    - **Same rules as buying:** each leg is built and checked by `/api/swap/build` (side `sell`, `source: 'basket'`), rebuilt before signing and sent only if it returns at least the card's "at least" USDC. A stop lists what was sold, offers "Review the rest", and says if one stock's approval is still open.
  - **Gates:** the same region gate and eligibility box as Execute.
  - **Stats:** builds carry `source: 'basket'`, so `/api/swap/stats` reports `bySource` (stock panel vs basket legs). The label is what the browser sends: it can't inflate counts or volume (those still need a mined, matching build), but a caller could mislabel their own swaps. Swaps logged before this have no source and count as `stock`.
- **Verified before launch (2026-10-13):** `eth_call` / `eth_simulateV1` of server-built buys and a sell with the Builder Code appended (fee reached the recipient: $0.20 on a $100 buy at 20 bp; nothing with the fee off); tampered calldata refused; a fake wallet in a real browser was asked for an exact approval (USDC, router, 100 USDC, Builder Code) and then the swap (to the router, Builder Code), at 390px light and desktop dark.

## Baskets (Basis Tilt)

`/baskets` offers **Afterbook Basis Tilt**: a basket of ten Base tokenized stocks (NVDAc, AAPLc, METAc, GOOGLc, AMZNc, MSFTc, MSTRc, SNDKc, SPCXc, TSLAc) weighted by what the on-chain books show instead of equally or by market cap. It runs on [Glider](https://glider.fi)'s B2B API: the user's funds sit in a smart account they own on Base, Glider's automation rebalances it (swaps route through LI.FI to Aerodrome), and Afterbook only chooses the target weights. Afterbook never holds funds, keys or executes trades for baskets. Not available in the US (the same best-effort, fail-closed `x-vercel-ip-country` gate as the rest of the app, applied to the page *and* to every `/api/baskets/*` route). No Afterbook fee.

**User flow** (`app/components/BasketPanel.tsx`): connect wallet → review and sign one message (creates the smart account; no funds move) → send USDC from the wallet to the account (an in-app plain ERC-20 transfer with a balance check and Max) → Glider buys the basket → withdraw any time, as USDC (Glider sells) or as the tokens, with an EIP-712 signature that expires in 10 minutes. Outcomes also show as toasts, and the withdrawal status is polled from Glider's operation endpoint. Enrolling requires ticking an acknowledgement linking to `/baskets/risks`.

**Weights** (`lib/baskets/weights.ts`, pure): half follows real pool depth, half follows |cash-vs-chain basis| counted only for liquid pools (≥ $1M; a thin pool's basis is mostly noise). Each name is bounded to 2–25%; bounds are enforced by scaling the raw scores with a bisection so the weights always sum to exactly 1, then rounded to 2-decimal percent strings that sum to exactly `100` (largest-remainder), which is the format Glider requires. `lib/baskets/tilt.ts` turns the tape into weights and CAIP-19 asset ids (`eip155:8453/erc20:0x…`).

**The basket universe is explicit.** `BASKET_SYMBOLS` in `lib/baskets/tilt.ts` is the ten names above and is deliberately separate from the tracked list in `lib/tokens.ts`: adding a stock to the tape must never silently change what enrolled users hold. Widening the basket is a deliberate decision plus a new strategy version.

**Smoothing** (`smoothWeights`): the published weights follow an exponential moving average (alpha 0.3) of the target, and a new Glider strategy version is published only when at least **10%** of the basket would change hands (`MIN_TURNOVER`; it was 5% until 2026-10-07, when a replay of 19 days of stored history showed 5% would have changed the basket on about 2 days in 3, roughly 90 bp a month in trading cost at ~0.9% per unit moved, versus about 3 publishes and ~50 bp at 10%) — every published version makes every enrolled portfolio trade (about 0.9% round trip in a measured $5 test), so a noisy basis must not cause daily churn. The daily job (`/api/cron/basket-tilt`, 22:00 UTC) saves the moving average on every scheduled run, publishes only when `BASKET_AUTOPUBLISH=1`, and messages `TELEGRAM_ADMIN_CHAT_ID` each time it publishes; `?dry=1` reports the plan, writes nothing, and shows `autopublishEnabled`.

**Strategy settings** (set once on the Glider strategy, not in code): private (`isPublic: false`), daily schedule, 1% slippage, 1% price impact, $1 minimum swap (Glider's own floor).

**Server routes** (`app/api/baskets/`): `enroll/signature`, `enroll`, `portfolio`, `start`, `withdraw/signature`, `withdraw`, `operation`. All go through `guardBasketRequest` (`lib/baskets/guard.ts`): fail-closed geo gate, config check (`GLIDER_API_KEY` and `GLIDER_STRATEGY_ID` both set — removing either is the off switch for the whole feature), then a Redis rate limiter that refuses when Redis is missing. The strategy and chain are fixed server-side, and a user's portfolio is always resolved from their owner address, never from a caller-supplied id. `lib/glider.ts` is the typed client (retries only on 429 and `API_506`; env values are trimmed because a stray space from a dashboard paste once broke enrollment on the live site).

**Daily job** (`/api/cron/basket-tilt`, 22:00 UTC): plans the next update and publishes only when `BASKET_AUTOPUBLISH=1` and `?dry=1` is not set; otherwise it just reports the plan and writes nothing. It refuses to touch a strategy whose asset list is not exactly the basket names. Auto-publish is off unless the variable is set, and **it is on in Production since 2026-10-07** (owner decision, after the threshold went to 10%); unsetting it and redeploying is the off switch (see the `basis-tilt-ops` skill).

**Environment variables**: `GLIDER_API_KEY` (a *production* tenant key — Glider's B2B API has no staging; test with separate keys. Production needs the six scopes `strategies:read/write`, `enroll:write`, `portfolios:read/write/withdraw`; a local dev key needs only `strategies:read`, `portfolios:read`, `enroll:write`), `GLIDER_API_BASE` (optional; defaults to `https://api.glider.fi/v2`), `GLIDER_STRATEGY_ID`, `BASKET_AUTOPUBLISH`, and `DEV_GEO_COUNTRY` (honoured only under `next dev`, so a developer machine can pass the fail-closed gate; a production build ignores it).

The link is built from the page's own origin, so previews only work on the live site. **Every share gets its own URL** (`?s=<stamp>`, set when the panel opens): X caches a link's preview card by URL for about a week and the card shows live numbers, so a bare `/MSTRc` kept showing the first card ever fetched, contradicting the post's sentence (seen 2026-10-07: post said 91.8 bp, cached card said +278.6 bp from two days earlier). Those URLs stay crawlable (X's and Telegram's preview crawlers obey `robots.txt`; a `Disallow: /*?s=` left every shared link on X without a card until 2026-10-13), and each page's canonical URL keeps them out of search results as duplicates. The link also carries the sharer's theme and, on `/today`, the stock on screen, and "Download card" is the same image as the preview. The text is the hero sentence's wording, facts only; keep it free of advice. The per-stock cards (`/card?page=stock&sym=…`) now carry the same icon and fade line as the `/today` card. The `/borrow` card (`page=borrow`, since 2026-10-14) shows the stock collateral and USDC borrowed across Aave and Morpho, and the four biggest markets with their Aero exit (Deep / Tight / Short and the amount, neutral colours). Its numbers and sentence come from one `getBorrowSummary()` read, `lib/borrowSummary.ts`, frozen in the snapshot's `borrow` field. **Share snapshots** (`lib/shareSnapshot.ts`, `POST /api/share`): when the panel opens, the server reads the tape once, writes the post's sentence from it (`lib/shareText.ts`, the same functions the page uses for its hero sentence) and stores that frozen tape in Redis for 30 days under the share's id, which becomes the link's `s`. The card for that link draws from the stored tape, so the sentence and the card always show the same number; the card's header says when ("Cash market open · 10:21 ET"). Before this, the sentence came from the screen and the card from live prices seconds later: a post said 8.0 bp over a +2.8 bp card. Numbers are computed on the server, never read from the link, so editing a URL can't put made-up numbers on a card. Rate limit 20 shares per 10 minutes per IP (`shareLimiter`). Until the snapshot is back the panel shows "Freezing the numbers…"; if it fails (no Redis, rate limit, 6 s), the button falls back to the on-screen sentence and a live card.
- **Brand mark:** a crescent (after hours) over two stepped lines (the order book). Defined once in `lib/brand.ts` and used by the header (`BrandMark.tsx`, themed: sky tile in dark mode, blue in light), the favicon (`app/icon.svg`, hand-copied from the same path) and the share cards (`lib/ogImage.tsx`). Apps that bookmark the site need raster icons (the Base app showed a letter "A" with only the SVG, 2026-10-18), so there are also PNGs rendered from `icon.svg`: `app/favicon.ico` (16/32/48), `app/icon1.png` (512, a PNG `<link rel="icon">`), `app/apple-icon.png` (180, iOS home screen) and `public/icon-192.png`/`icon-512.png` listed in the web app manifest (`app/manifest.ts`). If the mark changes, update `icon.svg` and re-render all of them.
- **Header** (`app/components/SiteHeader.tsx`): a full-width bar that condenses into a floating centred pill after about 32px of scroll (with hysteresis; desktop and tablet only — phones keep a two-row bar that scrolls away). The element keeps its height so nothing below shifts.
- **Token icons** (`lib/coinbaseIcons.ts`): the root layout reads Coinbase's public tokenized-stock list on the server (cached six hours), keeps an icon only when the entry's contract address matches `lib/tokens.ts` and the URL is on `metadata.coinbase.com`, and passes the URLs through context. `SymbolTile` renders the icon or a tinted letter tile (`lib/symbolStyle.ts`) when it is missing or fails. The icons are the companies' own logos served by Coinbase; the footer says they belong to their owners and Afterbook is not affiliated.
- **Trends** (`/api/sparklines`): one request for every row — up to 30 days of time-bucketed on-chain price samples plus the 24h change (reported only when a sample exists within 3 hours of 24 hours ago). All rows are drawn on one shared time axis whose length is the oldest stored sample (up to 30 days), and the tape header reads "Past Nd" with that real span, so it never claims 30 days before there are 30. A token with less than 3 days of its own history shows a small "New · Nd" label instead of a chart (a line stretched across the axis would pass for a long trend); from 3 days it draws only the part of the axis it covers. A token's 24h change uses the sample nearest to 24 hours ago within a 3-hour tolerance, so for a day-old token it is measured over slightly under 24 hours.
- **Form controls**: one zero-specificity rule styles every `select` and text input inside `<main>` (so components like the compact `.sticky-symbol-select` can still override it); the RainbowKit modal portals outside `<main>` and is untouched. Interactive elements without their own ring get a fallback `:focus-visible` outline.

## Security model

- Allowlisted contracts only — twelve token addresses, twelve pool addresses, all verified on-chain. The active stock's token and pool addresses are also shown directly in the Execute panel with BaseScan links, so you don't have to trust this README or leave the app to check what you're actually trading.
- All price/quote fetches happen server-side (Next.js route handlers); the browser only talks to this app's own `/api/*` routes.
- My Lots connects a wallet to *read* balances — no seed phrase ever touches this app and no signature or approval is ever requested there. Connecting only exposes the public address, which is already public on-chain regardless. **Baskets are different and say so:** enrolling asks the user to sign a message and withdrawing asks for an EIP-712 signature (both bound to the user's own account by Glider), and a deposit is a plain USDC transfer the user sends from their own wallet. Afterbook never holds funds or keys for baskets; Glider's automation can rebalance inside the strategy but, per Glider's docs, cannot withdraw without a fresh user signature.
- The non-US geofence (`x-vercel-ip-country`) is a best-effort UX gate, not a compliance control — it's defeated by any VPN, and the UI says so. Detected-US viewers see explicit "not available" copy rather than a silently-disabled button.
- **In-app swaps (since 2026-10).** Until then the README promised "no custom swap router, and this app never constructs swap calldata." Buy / Sell changed that, so the promise is now narrower and enforced in code (`lib/swap.ts`): Afterbook deploys no contract and holds no funds; KyberSwap builds the transaction and the server refuses it unless the decoded fields that decide the outcome match the request (router `0x6131…37b5`, input and output token, recipient = the connected wallet, amount and its split, a minimum received within 0.5% of the quote, Afterbook's fee or none, no permit; the route's internal steps are KyberSwap's, bounded by the router's minimum-received check); the wallet is only ever asked to approve that router for the exact amount, never unlimited; the review card shows the checked numbers before anything is signed. `checkSwapCalldata` is tested with tampered calldata (wrong recipient, lower minimum, other token, other fee recipient, a permit: all refused).
- "Or trade on Aerodrome's own site" still deep-links to `aerodrome.finance`, and Add liquidity is Aerodrome-only.
- Strict CSP (script-src limited to same-origin + a fresh per-request nonce — the tiny theme-init script in `<head>` carries that nonce), HSTS, X-Frame-Options: DENY, no third-party scripts or fonts (Poppins is self-hosted by `next/font`). The only third-party images are the token icons from `metadata.coinbase.com`, allowed in `img-src` and loaded with `referrerPolicy="no-referrer"`; each falls back to a letter tile if it fails.
- No user secrets anywhere. Server-side configuration is Redis (Upstash) credentials, the Telegram and CRON secrets, the x402 CDP keys, and the Glider tenant API key (`GLIDER_API_KEY`) — credentials for this *server*, never tied to a user or a wallet, and never sent to the browser (every `/api/baskets/*` route calls Glider from the server and resolves a user's portfolio from their address, never from a caller-supplied id). A full git-history secret scan was clean on 2026-10-13 (the only long hex strings are public transaction hashes). `npm audit --omit=dev` the same day: no critical, no high, 16 moderate. npm's suggested fix is wagmi 3, but RainbowKit (latest 2.2.11) only supports wagmi 2, so instead `package.json` `overrides` pin the patched versions inside the wallet libraries: `ws@8` → ^8.21.0 (the high: memory disclosure / DoS; WalletConnect's v7 copy is untouched, it isn't affected) and `uuid` → ^11.1.1. The 16 moderates left are one advisory, `decode-uri-component` 0.2.2 under WalletConnect's `query-string` 7: its fix (0.5.0) is ESM-only and would break that CommonJS `require`, and the worst case is a visitor's own tab hanging on a malformed WalletConnect link. They go away when RainbowKit supports wagmi 3. After touching the overrides, re-check the phone and desktop Connect wallet windows (`verify` skill). Next.js was moved to 16.3.8 the same day for a critical `next/og` advisory.

## Running it

Needs Node 20.9 or newer (Next.js 16).

```bash
npm install
npm run dev          # http://localhost:3000
npm run build        # the only automated check: typecheck + production build
```

No environment variables required to run it — the app works fully without them. `.env.example` lists every variable the code reads, empty; copy it to `.env.local` and fill only what you need. (`vercel env pull` would bring the project's real values, production keys included: read `CLAUDE.md` first.) Optionally:

- `KV_REST_API_URL` / `KV_REST_API_TOKEN` (or `UPSTASH_REDIS_REST_URL` / `UPSTASH_REDIS_REST_TOKEN`) — a Redis (Upstash) REST endpoint. Enables the "basis since close" sparkline's history, Telegram alerts, and the public API's key storage. Without it, the sparkline stays empty and both features return `503`.
- `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN`, `TELEGRAM_WEBHOOK_SECRET` — enables Telegram alerts. The token comes from [@BotFather](https://t.me/BotFather); the webhook secret is any random string you generate yourself, passed to Telegram's `setWebhook` as `secret_token` and checked against the `X-Telegram-Bot-Api-Secret-Token` header on every incoming update (in any built deployment the webhook refuses everything if it is unset). To confirm the bot's real username, call Telegram's `getMe` with the token (do not print the token).
- `CRON_SECRET` — a random string Vercel automatically attaches as `Authorization: Bearer <secret>` on cron-triggered requests ([documented pattern](https://vercel.com/docs/cron-jobs/manage-cron-jobs#securing-cron-jobs)); secures the cron routes against being triggered by anyone else.
- `TELEGRAM_ADMIN_CHAT_ID` — maintainer-only. A daily cron diffs Coinbase's public tokenized-stock list (`api.coinbase.com/v1/tokenized-stocks`, 106 names on 2026-10-08, 92 on 2026-10-07, 58 on 2026-10-01; the newest Aerodrome pools are still tiny (deepest $708), none near the $250k line; the older base.org/stocks page it used to scrape lists only ten) against the tickers tracked in `lib/tokens.ts`, checks each untracked one for an Aerodrome USDC pool and its real depth, and sends one Telegram digest to this chat ID (plus a ready-to-paste entry for any pool over $250k). A token is reported when it first has a pool and again when that pool first becomes liquid, never repeatedly. It never edits `lib/tokens.ts` itself — a human still reviews and adds each one. Also used to flag a real B20 `multiplier()` change (split or dividend) on any tracked stock.
- `GLIDER_API_KEY`, `GLIDER_STRATEGY_ID` (and optionally `GLIDER_API_BASE`, `BASKET_AUTOPUBLISH`) — enable Baskets; see "Baskets (Basis Tilt)". Without the first two the `/baskets` enroll panel stays off and every `/api/baskets/*` route answers `503`. `DEV_GEO_COUNTRY` (e.g. `DE`) works only under `next dev`.
- `SWAP_FEE_BPS`, `SWAP_FEE_RECIPIENT` — Afterbook's fee on in-app swaps (1–100 bp, and a public receiving address). The fee is off unless both are set and valid, and is always taken in USDC. See "In-app swaps".
- `CDP_API_KEY_ID`, `CDP_API_KEY_SECRET`, `X402_PAYOUT_ADDRESS` — enables the pay-per-call agent API (see "Pay-per-call agent API (x402)" below). The CDP keys are Coinbase Developer Platform *platform* credentials (from [portal.cdp.coinbase.com](https://portal.cdp.coinbase.com)), not a blockchain signing key — they authenticate this server to Coinbase's facilitator service, which is what actually settles payment on-chain. `X402_PAYOUT_ADDRESS` is any wallet address you control; USDC lands there directly. Without all three set, every `/api/v1/x402/*` route returns `503`.

## Public API

`/api/v1/tape` is a separate, versioned, documented contract for external consumers (bots, agents) — distinct from the unauthenticated `/api/tape` the site's own frontend polls, so internal refactors never silently break external callers.

Get a key (free, instant, no signup):
```bash
curl -X POST https://afterbook.app/api/v1/keys
```

Call the tape with it:
```bash
curl https://afterbook.app/api/v1/tape -H "Authorization: Bearer ab_..."
```

Response shape (frozen — internal-only fields like `cashTicker`/`name`/`cashStale` are deliberately not part of this contract):
```json
{
  "session": { "state": "after-hours", "label": "After-hours", "nyTime": "16:30" },
  "asOf": 1735000000000,
  "rows": [
    { "symbol": "NVDAc", "cashUsd": 218.36, "midUsd": 218.65, "basisBp": 13.5, "depthUsd": 2500000 }
  ]
}
```

Rate limit: 1 request/second per key.

## Agent / MCP

`https://afterbook.app/api/mcp` is a remote [MCP](https://modelcontextprotocol.io) server for agents (Claude, etc.) — no key, no signup, read-only. Add it as a remote MCP server in Claude Code, Claude Desktop, or any MCP-compatible client pointed at that URL.

Three tools:

- **`get_tape`** — no input. Same data as `/api/v1/tape` above.
- **`get_quote`** — `{ symbol, usdcIn }`. Shares out, execution price, and price impact for sizing a USDC → stock trade — the same estimate Lot Lab shows, not a firm quote; the actual fill always happens on Aerodrome's own app.
- **`get_basis_stats`** — `{ symbol }`. Mean/mean-absolute/max-absolute basis in bp while the cash market was closed, over the last 30 days of sampled history. `null` if there isn't enough history yet.

## Pay-per-call agent API (x402)

`/api/v1/x402/tape` and `/api/v1/x402/quote` are the same data as `/api/v1/tape` and `/api/quote` above, gated by the [x402](https://x402.org) HTTP micropayment protocol instead of an API key — $0.02 in USDC per call on Base, paid directly from an agent's own wallet via [EIP-3009](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-3009) `transferWithAuthorization`. No signup, no key, no rate-limit ceiling — payment itself is the throttle. Same $0.02 tier: **`/api/v1/x402/execute-link`** — `{ symbol, usdcIn, direction?: 'buy' | 'sell' }`, a sized trade estimate (same shape as `/api/v1/x402/quote`) plus a verified Aerodrome deep link for that exact direction. Deliberately a *link*, not raw calldata: this app has no confirmed-working router for these Slipstream pools outside Aerodrome's own frontend (their documented public SwapRouter/Quoter are bound to a different CL factory and revert on these specific pools; their Universal Router shows zero real usage against these pools in a live on-chain sample; their classic `Router.sol` is built for the old stable/volatile AMM pools, not Slipstream — all checked directly on-chain before deciding not to ship calldata construction). An agent's own execution layer opens/submits the link; this route never returns calldata or touches a key. (The site's own Buy / Sell, added later, does route these pools, through KyberSwap's aggregator with its calldata checked before signing — see "In-app swaps"; it is a browser flow, not part of this API.)

**This isn't exclusive data** — `/api/quote` and every MCP tool above stay fully free and unauthenticated, on purpose. This tier's actual value is zero setup friction for an agent that would rather pay $0.02 than provision a key and live under a rate limit.

Three higher, $0.05 endpoints for agents doing heavier analysis:

- **`/api/v1/x402/history`** — `{ symbol }`. 30 days of sampled basis history (same data `/api/history` serves free) plus the pool's current on-chain tick-liquidity distribution (same data `/api/depth` serves free) — bundled into one call for backtesting agents that want both the historical mispricing signal and how thick the book was at the time.
- **`/api/v1/x402/lp-range`** — `{ symbol, holdingDays?, targetProbabilityPct? }`. A recommended concentrated-liquidity tick range sized from realized volatility over a target holding period (default 7 days, 70% coverage), widened when the symbol's next earnings date falls inside that window. This one has no free equivalent — it's genuinely new derived output, not a convenience bundle of already-free routes. The earnings widening is a documented flat heuristic (no options-implied-vol data source exists in this app), not a real implied move. Also returns `divergenceLossAtBoundary` — the impermanent-loss benchmark at each edge of that range (`lib/lpRange.ts`).
- **`/api/v1/x402/liquidation-capacity`** — `{ symbol, collateralShares?, borrowUsd?, marketId? }`. The `/borrow` numbers for one stock: its Morpho and Aave V4 USDC markets (each with `venue`; Aave ids are `aave:<reserveId>`, and `borrowUsd` is null because the pool is shared), the Aero-vs-oracle gap, the largest liquidation Aerodrome can absorb at a profit, a sell-side impact curve, and (with a position) its liquidation price and whether that liquidation could be sold on Aerodrome. `marketId` defaults to the market holding the most collateral. $0.05.

**Afterbook never holds a signing key for this.** Settlement is executed by Coinbase's hosted CDP facilitator, not by this app — Afterbook only supplies a plain receiving wallet address (`X402_PAYOUT_ADDRESS`) and a CDP *platform* API key/secret pair that authenticates this server to Coinbase's facilitator service (not a blockchain key). Payment lands directly in that address (the owner's own wallet); nothing is ever custodied here, same as everywhere else in this app, and nothing swaps or spends it automatically.

All six routes 503 with `{ "error": "x402 tier not configured" }` until `CDP_API_KEY_ID`, `CDP_API_KEY_SECRET`, and `X402_PAYOUT_ADDRESS` are all set.
